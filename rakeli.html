About Our Institution

Fostering Excellence, Character & Innovation

Our History & Legacy

Established in 2023, Rakeli International School was founded to fill the need for high-quality, holistic basic education in the rapidly growing community of Adjen-Kotoku and surrounding areas in Ga West Municipality, Accra.

From modern classroom architecture and state-of-the-art ICT labs to dedicated educators, we have grown into a preferred institution where academic discipline meets creative nurturing.

Our Mission

To provide accessible, high-quality, holistic basic education that empowers students with knowledge, moral integrity, and modern technological skills.

Our Vision

To be a premier basic school in Accra recognized for academic distinction, innovative teaching, and producing leaders ready to strive for the crown in all endeavors.

Admissions Process

4 Simple Steps to Join Rakeli

1

Obtain & Submit Application Form

Pick up an admission form at our Adjen-Kotoku campus or fill out our online inquiry form below.

2

Placement Assessment

Prospective students undergo an age-appropriate readiness evaluation in Mathematics and English.

3

Parental Interaction

A brief meeting between school leadership and parents to align on values and student support strategies.

4

Official Enrollment

Upon acceptance, parents receive fee schedules and uniform guidelines to complete enrollment.
